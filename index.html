Important accuracy note

Historical hit rate is a simple candle-based backtest, not a guarantee of future results or a broker-verified payout result. Real execution can differ because of spread, entry timing, price movement, connection delay and contract settlement rules. The six indicators are partly correlated. Do not treat indicator agreement as a probability of winning; test on a demo account first.
