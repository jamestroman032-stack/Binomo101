How this bot decides

It builds one-minute candles from Deriv EUR/USD ticks, then scores Bollinger Bands, Supertrend, EMA/SMA, RSI, Stochastic and MACD as bullish or bearish votes. ATR is a volatility/suitability filter, not a direction vote. It returns UP or DOWN only when the chosen vote threshold is met and volatility is not unusually low or high; otherwise it returns WAIT.

Important: This is an experimental technical-analysis tool, not a validated profitable strategy. Short-expiry binary options are extremely risky. Indicator agreement and the displayed score are not calibrated win probabilities. Test on demo, include payout/breakeven math, and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
